# VerifiAI - Supported AI Models & Providers

## 1. Groq AI Models (Primary Fast Inference Provider)
- llama-3.1-8b-instant (Default fast reasoning model)
- llama-3.3-70b-versatile (High accuracy multi-step reasoning fallback)
- mixtral-8x7b-32768 (MoE context architecture fallback)

## 2. Google Gemini Models (Optional AI Provider)
- gemini-1.5-flash (Fast lightweight summary generator)
- gemini-1.5-pro (Deep factual analysis)

## 3. Python Local Machine Learning Service
- Logistic Regression Classifier with TF-IDF Vectorization
- Models stored in `ml_service/models/model.pkl` and `ml_service/models/vectorizer.pkl`
